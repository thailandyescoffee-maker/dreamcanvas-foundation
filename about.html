Our Story

A journey of love, faith, and shared purpose.

Where It Began

2012 · A Small Beginning

Families and friends who found us simply called it YES School. As we grew, our name changed to YES Education Center, but our purpose never changed. Volunteers and supporters from near and far joined us along the way, united by a shared commitment to children and their future.

2026 · A New Chapter

That commitment now continues as Dream Canvas Foundation. Learning Support. Digital Learning. Community. Hope for the Future. One purpose remains: helping every child dream, grow, and build a brighter future.
