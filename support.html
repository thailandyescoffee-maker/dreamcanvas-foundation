Frequently Asked Questions

How can I donate?

You can make a one-time or monthly donation through our secure online form, or reach out to us directly for other giving options.

Can I support from overseas?

Yes. We welcome support from friends around the world, and international donations are gratefully accepted.

How are donations used?

Every gift goes directly toward learning support, essential resources, and community programs for children, with transparent reporting on how funds are used.

Will I receive updates?

Yes. Supporters receive updates on our programs and the difference their generosity is making in children's lives.
